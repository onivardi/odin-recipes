Million Dollar Cucumber Salad

Home

This million dollar cucumber salad, lightly dressed, is crisp, creamy, loaded with flavor, and perfect for a summer picnic or potluck. Best if served immediately. It's addicting so I recommend doubling the recipe if taking to a gathering.

Ingredients

  • 2 English cucumbers, sliced into 1/4-inch rounds
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon freeze-dried dill
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 1/3 cup real bacon bits
  • 1/2 cup ranch salad dressing, such as Wishbone
  • 1/2 cup shredded sharp Cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Place sliced cucumbers, garlic powder, dill, salt and pepper in a bowl. Toss to coat.
  2. Add green onions, bacon bits, and ranch salad dressing; toss to coat.
  3. Add shredded cheese and toss one last time. Serve immediately.
